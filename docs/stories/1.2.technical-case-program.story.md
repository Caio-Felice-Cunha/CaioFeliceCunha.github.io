## Story

**As a** recruiter or technical peer, **I want** every released project to expose both an immediate demo and a meaningful engineering explanation, **so that** I can evaluate Caio's product and implementation depth within two clicks.

1. Scoopy, Supply Chain, DrumAI, MorarFora, and all three schedulers publish the approved technical cases.
2. Project cards expose distinct demo and engineering-case actions with truthful status copy.
3. The portfolio and profile contain no unsupported metrics, credentials, PII, client material, excluded-project references, or broken links.
4. Central desktop/mobile smoke tests cover every public demo and engineering path.
5. Publication occurs in four gated waves: Scoopy/Supply, DrumAI/MorarFora, schedulers, portfolio/profile.
